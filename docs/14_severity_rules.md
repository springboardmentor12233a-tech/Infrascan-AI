# Advisory Damage Severity Assessment Specification & Engineering Rules

> **CRITICAL DISCLAIMER**  
> **Advisory result for engineer review. Not a structural safety verdict. Critical means urgent engineering review.**  
> The severity score and priority classification generated by InfraScan-AI are strictly advisory decision-support signals designed to assist licensed civil engineers during structural triage. They do NOT replace physical inspections, non-destructive testing (NDT), or formal structural safety engineering evaluations.

---

## 1. Overview and Design Principles

The InfraScan damage severity assessment system converts raw machine learning outputs (road distress bounding boxes from YOLOv8s and pixel-level crack segmentation skeletons from U-Net++ ResNet-34) into an interpretable, transparent, and editable engineering severity rating.

### Core Architecture Principles:
1. **Explainable and Fully Decomposed**: Every severity grade is computed as the sum of explicit, transparent factor points (Factors A through F). The exact factor breakdown is displayed to engineers as "Why this severity".
2. **Deterministic and Pure**: The assessment algorithm is stateless and deterministic—identical inputs produce identical scores and classifications without stochastic jitter.
3. **Conservative Safety Guardrails**: Hard overrides ("forced minimums") prevent severe structural defects (such as exposed reinforcement bars or substantial spallation) from being masked by low crack density.
4. **Separation of Physical vs. Digital Scales**: Millimetre width points are scored **only** when an image has a calibrated physical scale. When no scale exists, metric width points are 0 and marked `"width not scored: no scale"`, while pixel measurements remain visible and unhidden.
5. **Human-in-the-Loop Governance**: AI severities are categorized as `Advisory (Awaiting Review)` and are **never displayed as "final"** until an authorized civil engineer or administrator reviews, confirms, or corrects them with mandatory audit logging.

## 2. Severity Scoring Formulation

The advisory severity score is the sum of points across six independent factors, capped at 100:

$$\text{Raw Score} = P_{\text{density}} + P_{\text{length}} + P_{\text{branching}} + P_{\text{type}} + P_{\text{area}} + P_{\text{width}}$$

$$\text{Final Score} = \min(100.0, \text{Raw Score})$$

### Score Bands and Severity Classification

| Score Band | Advisory Severity Level | Color Coding | Suggested Priority | Operational Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0.0 – < 10.0** | `none` | Grey (`#6B7280`) | `routine_monitoring` | Logged during routine maintenance inspection |
| **10.0 – < 30.0** | `minor` | Green (`#10B981`) | `low` | Monitor in subsequent scheduled inspection cycle |
| **30.0 – < 55.0** | `moderate` | Yellow (`#F59E0B`) | `medium` | Plan localized surface sealing or preventive maintenance |
| **55.0 – < 80.0** | `severe` | Orange (`#F97316`) | `high` | Prioritized field inspection by senior engineer |
| **80.0 – 100.0** | `critical` | Red (`#EF4444`) | `urgent_engineering_review` | Immediate physical inspection and safety barrier review |

## 4. Forced Safety Minimums & Priority Elevation

### Forced Minimum Overrides
Even if total factor points evaluate to `none`, `minor`, or `moderate`, specific high-risk distresses trigger mandatory minimum elevation to at least **`severe`**:
1. **Exposed Reinforcing Steel (`exposed_bars`)**: Any detection with $\text{confidence} \ge 0.50$ forces severity to at least `severe` due to imminent risk of rebar section loss and bond failure.
2. **Substantial Surface Loss**: Any detection of `spallation`, `pothole`, or `D40` with `damage_area_share >= 10%` forces severity to at least `severe`.

### Asset Importance Elevation
Structural consequence varies by asset criticality:
- Standard mapping: `none` $\to$ `routine_monitoring`, `minor` $\to$ `low`, `moderate` $\to$ `medium`, `severe` $\to$ `high`, `critical` $\to$ `urgent_engineering_review`.
- When an asset's `importance_level` is **`high`** (e.g. strategic highway bridges, transit tunnels), the suggested priority is **elevated by one level** (capped at `urgent_engineering_review`).
- Asset construction material is **not** used to elevate severity.

### Quality and Confidence Review Flags
- `needs_review`: Always `True` upon automated AI evaluation until explicitly confirmed by an engineer.
- `low_confidence_review`: Flagged as `True` if any qualifying detection used in scoring has $\text{confidence} < 0.40$ or the image quality check has `quality_status == "warning"` (e.g. blur or sub-optimal lighting).

## 5. Technical Limitations & Physical Bounds

Deploying automated AI severity grading in civil infrastructure requires explicit recognition of physical, mathematical, and observational boundaries:

> [!WARNING]
> 1. **Engineer-Defined Placeholders**: Current factor point values and width thresholds are engineering placeholders. They have not yet been calibrated or certified against a specific regional design standard (such as ACI 224.1R, AASHTO, or Eurocode 2).
> 2. **YOLOv8s Recall (~0.45)**: The baseline multi-class detector exhibits a validation recall of ~0.45 on the RDD2022 benchmark. This means approximately 55% of localized distress boxes may not be detected by the AI. An image with zero detections does **not** prove the absence of distress; it merely reflects that no defects surpassed the confidence threshold.
> 3. **Sub-Pixel Caliper Uncertainty (~4.7 px)**: The Euclidean Distance Transform (EDT2) algorithm evaluates pixel-level crack width. Empirical validation demonstrates an average uncertainty of $\pm 4.7$ pixels due to edge gradients and illumination falloff. In low-resolution photos (e.g., $1.5\text{ mm/px}$), this translates to an uncertainty margin of $\pm 7\text{ mm}$.
> 4. **Distribution Shift**: The baseline models were trained on asphalt road pavement images (RDD2022, Crack500). Concrete surfaces (bridge piers, pre-stressed girders, retaining walls) exhibit different texture, efflorescence, formwork seams, and aggregate exposure that may generate false positives or false negatives.
> 5. **Camera Perspective**: Two-dimensional homography and millimeter scaling assume a planar surface viewed perpendicular to the optical axis. Oblique viewpoints distort spatial density and width.

## 7. How to Edit and Update Severity Rules

The severity configuration is stored in `backend/app/config/severity_rules.json`. Civil engineers and administrators can adjust point allocations, add new distress classes, or modify score bands without touching Python code.

### Rules Schema (`backend/app/config/severity_rules.json`)
```json
{
  "rules_version": "1.0.0",
  "status": "engineer-defined placeholders, not validated against an engineering standard",
  "bands": [
    {"min_score": 0.0, "max_score": 10.0, "severity": "none"},
    {"min_score": 10.0, "max_score": 30.0, "severity": "minor"},
    {"min_score": 30.0, "max_score": 55.0, "severity": "moderate"},
    {"min_score": 55.0, "max_score": 80.0, "severity": "severe"},
    {"min_score": 80.0, "max_score": 100.0, "severity": "critical"}
  ],
  "factors": {
    "crack_density_percent": { ... },
    "relative_length": { ... },
    "branching": { ... },
    "damage_type": { ... },
    "damage_area_share": { ... },
    "width_mm": { ... }
  },
  "forced_minimums": [ ... ],
  "priority_mapping": { ... }
}
```

### Editing Guidelines & Integrity Rules:
1. **Band Continuity**: Bands must start at `0.0` and end at `100.0` with no gaps and no overlapping intervals. `load_severity_rules()` automatically validates continuity and raises a `ValueError` on gap or overlap.
2. **Non-Negative Points**: All point values and weights must be $\ge 0.0$. Negative values are strictly rejected.
3. **Cryptographic Versioning**: The backend calculates the SHA-256 hash of the rules file on every load and records it on every `SeverityAssessment` record for compliance and legal traceability.
4. **Recomputing Existing Images**: After editing `severity_rules.json`, existing images can be updated using the UI "Recompute" button or by sending:
   ```http
   POST /images/{id}/severity/recompute
   Authorization: Bearer <token>
   ```
   This recalculates factor points under the updated rules without re-running heavy GPU inference, preserving historical assessments with `is_superseded = True`.
